## 2024-05-16 - Add missing aria-labels to utility buttons
**Learning:** Generic utility buttons in Flow Studio lack context-specific aria-labels, potentially confusing screen reader users. Small elements like copy or reload buttons need clear semantic names indicating what exactly they interact with.
**Action:** Ensure that all newly added utility buttons, particularly those represented primarily by icons or generic text ('Copy', 'Reload'), explicitly specify an aria-label with their specific context.
